# Interview Demo Path (~2 minutes)

Not date-dependent. Launch from the app: **Interview Demo Path** button or `/?demo=1`.

## Sequence

### 0:00–0:30 — Executive X-ray (`/`)
Say: O5 is a licensing/operating company, not simply a retailer. Associated brand names ≠ ownership. The job likely touches a narrower set of O5-operated DTC systems—especially Quiksilver and Billabong after Liberated—while Eddie Bauer’s seat ownership is unresolved.

### 0:30–1:00 — Portfolio & Properties (`/portfolio`)
Show the relationship chain and table columns. Point at Quik/Billabong as likely relevant; Eddie Bauer as associated/unclear; Dickies etc. as broader—not paid-media ownership.

### 1:00–2:00 — Feed Control Room (`/feed#control-room`)
Show the permanent illustrative banner. Filter margin/inventory/promo. Explain the loop: product data → feed → Merchant Center → Shopping/PMax → merch/inventory → feed updates. Then **stop**.
