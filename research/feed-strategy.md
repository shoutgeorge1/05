# Feed strategy

Illustrative model in app Feed Lab (`src/data/feeds.ts`).

Priorities for O5-like portfolio:

1. Availability truth vs OMS
2. Apparel attributes (gender, size, color, GTIN)
3. Title normalization
4. sale_price aligned to full-price brand policy
5. Custom labels for priority, margin proxy, inventory, newness, promo
6. Brand-separated campaign consumption of shared standards
