# Core operating thesis

O5 Group appears to be a private apparel licensing and operating company. Associated brand names are not automatically O5-owned, and O5-operated DTC storefronts are not automatically in-scope for every performance-marketing seat.

**For the Paid Search and Shopping Specialist role, the useful problem is narrower:**

1. Confirm which storefronts and advertising accounts the seat owns.
2. Validate conversion and Merchant Center / feed integrity on those surfaces.
3. Connect media decisions to inventory, merchandising, promotions, and product economics.

Quiksilver and Billabong are the strongest publicly supported likely-relevant DTC surfaces because of the Liberated Brands → O5 transition reporting plus live O5 legal/Shopify signatures.

Eddie Bauer is confirmed under OUTDOOR 5, LLC as an O5-related DTC property; whether this seat owns EB paid media remains unresolved.

Legacy Liberated references are **observed inheritance / transition signals**, not proof that systems are broken or that Ads accounts transferred.
